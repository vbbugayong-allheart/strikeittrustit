Hole 1

Title, description & goal

Build a more consistent golf game by improving my ball contact and my mental game, and create an interactive website that inspires other golfers to do the same.

Why I changed topic

My first idea was to design a sportswear shirt. Once I started, I realised I wasn't motivated by it, and a project I don't care about is hard to keep going for months. Golf is something I already spend time on and genuinely want to get better at, so I switched.

My short game is already pretty decent. What makes my scores jump around is inconsistent ball contact (fat and thin shots) and losing my focus after a bad shot or when I feel pressure. Those are the two things I'm working on.

What I'm making

  • Product: this interactive website, part process journal and part training tool.
  • Inside it: two mini-games that train timing and composure under pressure, a breathing reset routine, my research, my stats, and a weekly journal.
  • Purpose: to inspire golfers of any level to work on their contact and their mindset, and to show that improvement comes from habits, not luck.
Hole 2

Learning goal & outcome

Learning goal

Learn how to strike the ball consistently and how to manage pressure and emotions on the course, and learn to share that through an interactive website.

I want to understand what causes fat and thin shots and how to control the low point of my swing. I also want to learn practical mental skills, like a pre-shot routine, breathing and resetting after a bad shot, and how to design a website that people actually enjoy using.

Intended outcome
  • More solid, centered strikes and fewer fat or thin shots.
  • A pre-shot routine and a reset routine I use every round.
  • Fewer "blow-up" holes after a mistake, and steadier scores.
  • A finished interactive website that visitors say inspires them.

ATL skills: research, self-management (routine and emotional control), communication (website design), reflection (weekly journal).

Hole 6

Research: contact and the mental game

What I've found so far. Sources will be added as my research continues.

Low point control

Fat and thin shots both come from the bottom of the swing arc being in the wrong place. Good iron players bottom out just after the ball.

Centered strike

Hitting the middle of the face gives more distance and straighter shots. Strike spray or foot powder shows exactly where you're making contact.

Pressure and the body

Nerves raise your heart rate and tighten muscles, which speeds up tempo and throws off contact. Slow breathing helps bring the body back to calm.

Routine and reset

A repeated pre-shot routine gives the mind something steady to focus on, and a reset routine stops one bad shot from turning into a bad hole.

Week of Nov 20

Switching topics and finding my real focus

This week I dropped my sportswear shirt project and switched to golf. At first I thought I'd work on my short game, but when I looked honestly at my rounds, my short game is fine. My scores swing because of poor contact and because I get frustrated or nervous and let one bad shot turn into a bad hole.

I decided my website should be interactive, so visitors can feel what pressure does to timing, and I want it to inspire other golfers. Next steps: present at the work-in-progress fair, research low point control and mental skills, and record my baseline stats.
